Open **`6_Member_Team_Presentation_Guide.pdf`** in `A_Video_Presentation_Assets/`.

- **Member 1 (0:00 – 1:40):** Slides 1 & 2 (Introduction & Original Paper's Vision)
- **Member 2 (1:40 – 3:20):** Slides 3 & 4 (Published Results Audit & Side-by-Side Comparison Table)
- **Member 3 (3:20 – 5:00):** Slides 5 & 6 (The Two Big Problems & Our Multi-Scale Solution)
- **Member 4 (5:00 – 6:30):** Slides 7 & 8 (Visual Proof on Bird #3 & Faithfulness Perturbation Game)
- **Member 5 (6:30 – 8:15):** Slides 9 & 10 (Distance Metric Ablation & Speed/Memory Profile)
- **Member 6 (8:15 – 10:00):** Slides 11 & 12 (Summary of Achievements & Conclusion / Q&A)

Each member's section tells them **exactly what simple words to say to Sir** and provides the answer to the question Sir is most likely to ask!
