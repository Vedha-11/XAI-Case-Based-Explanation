## 2. Presentation Formats Provided in this Folder

You have two ready-to-use presentation options:

### Option 1: Microsoft PowerPoint (`Phase_2_Project_Presentation.pptx`)
- Open `Phase_2_Project_Presentation.pptx` (also saved as `Phase_2_Presentation_Final.pptx`).
- Every single slide includes complete **Presenter Speaker Notes** at the bottom explaining what to say to the professor.
- All high-resolution figures, paper extracts, and before-and-after tables are formatted and embedded.

### Option 2: Interactive Web Slides (`presentation_slides.html`)
- Double-click `presentation_slides.html` to open it in Chrome or Edge.
- Press `F11` on Windows for **Full-Screen Mode**.
- Navigate using **Left/Right Arrow keys** or Spacebar.
- Click **"Toggle Speaker Notes"** to display what to say on-screen during practice.

## 3. How to Record Your Screen and Voice

You can use any of these free methods to record your screen and microphone:

### Method A: Windows Game Bar (Built-in, No Installation Needed)
1. Open PowerPoint or Chrome (`presentation_slides.html`) in full screen.
2. Press `Win + G` on your keyboard to open Xbox Game Bar.
3. Make sure your microphone is unmuted (mic icon active).
4. Click the **Record** button (or press `Win + Alt + R`).
5. Speak through the slides following the word-for-word narration in `10_Minute_Presentation_Script.md`.
6. Press `Win + Alt + R` to stop recording. The MP4 video will be saved in `Videos\Captures`.

### Method B: OBS Studio / Zoom
- **Zoom:** Start a personal meeting $\rightarrow$ Click **Share Screen** (select PowerPoint/Browser) $\rightarrow$ Click **Record to Computer**.
- **OBS Studio:** Add a Display Capture source, verify mic levels, and click "Start Recording".

## 4. Google Drive Submission Instructions

1. Verify that your recorded video is **10:00 or less** in duration.
2. Upload the MP4 video to your personal Google Drive.
3. Right-click the uploaded video $\rightarrow$ Click **Share** $\rightarrow$ Under *General access*, select **"Anyone with the link can view"**.
4. Copy the link and paste it into the Google Form submission field for Deliverable A.
